# Hooks and Content Pillars

## Content pillars

- Form tutorials: one exercise, correct form, the top mistakes, one regression and one progression.

- Mistake and myth videos: one wrong belief, a clear demo of wrong versus right, one fix the viewer can try today.

- Routines: one session or one body part, with exercise order, sets, reps, and rest in the caption rather than spoken fast.

- Progress and transformation: honest timelines, what changed in training or habits, no promised results for viewers.

- Beginner guides: first day in the gym, machine setup, gym etiquette, simple words for every term.

- Nutrition basics: general principles such as protein at each meal or hydration, with no personal prescriptions.

## Hook formulas for the first 2 seconds

- Stop doing [exercise] like this.

- Three mistakes killing your [exercise].

- You only need [number] exercises for [goal].

- Do this before every [workout type] session.

- Beginners, fix this first.

- Show the wrong form silently, freeze, then speak the fix.

## Format picker

- Pick a mistake format when the topic names one exercise and a common error.

- Pick a routine format when the topic names a day, a body part, or a goal with a time limit.

- Pick a myth format when the topic is a belief, a fear, or a too good claim.

- Pick a beginner guide when the audience is new and the win is confidence rather than performance.
