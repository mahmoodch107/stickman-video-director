# Gym Characters — Locked (2.5D Sketch Duo)

## Style anchor (repeat verbatim in every Mode B prompt)

- Hand-drawn 2.5D pencil sketch animation, grey shading everywhere including the gym background; the only colours in the whole video are the Student's bright orange shorts and the bright red muscle glow on the muscle being trained. No photorealistic people, no colour on the Coach or the gym, no visible text or logos.

## Coach anchor (repeat verbatim in every Mode B prompt)

- A very muscular bulky gym coach with short cropped dark hair and a serious strong face that smiles when encouraging, drawn in grey pencil sketch, wearing a dark grey tank top, black shorts, white socks and dark trainers. He teaches, points at the working muscle, corrects form and celebrates the fix with a thumbs up.

## Student anchor (repeat verbatim in every Mode B prompt)

- A lean athletic young man with messy dark hair and a calm friendly face, grey pencil sketch body, shirtless, wearing bright orange shorts and white trainers. He demonstrates every exercise; the muscle he is training glows bright red with visible anatomy detail while it works, and the glow fades when the muscle rests.

## Identity

- Coach (Mahmood can rename him at any time): the teacher and narrator of every AI generated gym video. Strong and intense in look, warm and encouraging in words — he never shames the viewer. He shows the mistake call-out, the cues and the celebration.

- Student (Mahmood can rename him at any time): the beginner who demonstrates. He first shows the common mistake, then the correct form, so viewers see their own error and the fix on the same body.

- These are the only two characters by default. Add another character only when Mahmood asks for one for a specific video.

## Muscle glow rule

- Only the muscle being trained glows, only on the Student, and only while that muscle is working. The glow is the teaching highlight of the channel: scripts should name the target muscle in the spoken line when the glow appears.

## Voice blocks (frozen, repeat verbatim in every Mode B prompt)

- Coach voice: one deep adult male narrator, strong and warm coaching tone, clear pronunciation, natural pace, same voice, same accent and same recording style in every clip and every video. He speaks almost all lines.

- Student voice: one young adult male voice, friendly and lighter than the Coach, used only for a short question or reaction line when the script calls for it, same voice in every clip.

## Design rules

- Keep both faces, hair, builds and outfits identical in every appearance. Coach stays fully grey; Student keeps the orange shorts, and red appears only as the muscle glow.

- Keep the 2.5D hand-drawn sketch style in every generation. Never render them photorealistic, as flat stick figures, or in full colour, and add that ban to every prompt's negative instructions.

- Show form with the Student's whole body visible. For a form demo use a side view, for a mistake reveal use a front view, and for the fix celebration use a front view with the Coach giving a thumbs up.

- No costume changes, no logos and no visible words on clothing.
