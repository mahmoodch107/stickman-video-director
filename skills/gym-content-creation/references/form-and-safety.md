# Form and Safety

## Form cue rules

- Teach the setup before the movement, covering stance or grip, bracing, and the start position.

- Give at most three cues per exercise in a short video and phrase each cue as one action the viewer can feel or see.

- Show a slow controlled rep first, then a normal speed rep.

- Name a beginner regression for every loaded or complex movement, such as a box squat, a wall push up, or a lighter weight with a shorter range.

## Safety boundaries

- Never tell a viewer to train through sharp pain, numbness, or dizziness.

- Never diagnose an injury or promise that an exercise fixes pain.

- Never promise spot fat loss, a cure, or a guaranteed amount of weight or muscle gain in a set time.

- Keep nutrition general and educational, with no personal calorie targets, meal prescriptions, or supplement dosing.

- Recommend a qualified coach, physiotherapist, or doctor when a topic involves pain, injury, a medical condition, pregnancy, or disordered eating.

- Prefer well established coaching cues and exercises, and leave out a movement rather than guessing at its safe teaching points.
