# Captions and Publishing Kit

## What the kit delivers

- Two title options, one direct and one curiosity based, each under 60 characters where the platform allows.

- One cover or thumbnail idea with 2 to 4 large words and the hero pose or hero frame described.

- A caption with the takeaway in the first line, the sets and reps or the fix steps in the middle, and one question to invite comments.

- Three to five relevant hashtags, with no hashtag stuffing and no banned or misleading tags.

- One call to action, chosen from save this, share with a training partner, follow for the next part, or comment your exercise for the next video.

## Caption template

- Line 1: the takeaway or the mistake in plain words.

- Middle: numbered steps, cues, or the routine with sets, reps, and rest.

- Last line: one question for the comments.

- Final line: hashtags on their own line.
