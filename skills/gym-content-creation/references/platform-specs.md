# Platform Specs

## Short vertical video

- Use 9:16 for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts unless the user asks otherwise.

- Keep the face, the working muscle, and the equipment inside the centre of the frame and away from the bottom caption zone and the right side buttons.

- Target 15 to 60 seconds for short form, with roughly 2 to 3 spoken words per second.

- Plan a visual change every 2 to 3 seconds, such as an angle change, a zoom, a wrong versus right cut, or a close up of grip or foot position.

## Long form

- Use 16:9 for a full YouTube tutorial and chapter the script by exercise or by mistake.

- Open with the result or the problem, then the plan for the video, then the teaching sections.

## Captions and text

- Write captions for silent viewing, because many viewers watch with sound off.

- Keep on-screen text to 2 to 5 words per overlay and list overlays as post-production notes, never as text inside a generated clip.

- Put sets, reps, and rest times in the caption or a pinned comment, not in fast spoken lines.
