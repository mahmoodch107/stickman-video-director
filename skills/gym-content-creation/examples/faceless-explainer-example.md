# Example, Mode B Faceless Explainer

## Brief

- Topic: why your muscles are sore after a new workout.

- Platform: YouTube Short, 9:16, 40 seconds, stickman style from the directing_stickman_videos skill.

- Takeaway: soreness after new movement is common, it is not a score for a good workout, and light movement and sleep help more than training through it.

## Phase A outline

- Hook, 0 to 2s: a stickman tries to sit on a chair the day after leg day and freezes halfway down.

- Script beats: what soreness is in plain words, why new exercises cause more of it, why soreness alone does not prove progress, and what to do instead, which is walk, sleep, and train the muscle again when ready.

- Clips: four clips of about 10 seconds, one idea per clip, same character anchor and same narrator voice in every clip.

- Safety note in the caption: sharp pain, swelling, or pain that does not ease in a few days is a reason to see a qualified professional.

- Call to action: comment the exercise that makes you sorest.
