# Example, Mode A Filmed Reel

## Brief

- Topic: three push up mistakes for beginners.

- Platform: Instagram Reel, 9:16, 35 seconds.

- Takeaway: a straight line from head to heels, hands under shoulders, full range.

## Phase A outline

- Hook, 0 to 2s: trainer holds a sagging hips push up, freezes, and says stop doing push ups like this.

- Script beats: mistake one hips sag, fix brace and squeeze glutes. Mistake two hands too far forward, fix hands under shoulders. Mistake three half reps, fix chest close to the floor or use a wall regression.

- Shots: side angle for back line, front angle for hand position, side close angle for range, one clean set of five reps to finish.

- On-screen text: Fix your push up, Hips up, Hands here, Full range.

- Call to action: save this for your next session.
