# 30 Day Content Calendar Template

## Weekly rhythm

- Monday: one form tutorial.

- Tuesday: one mistake or myth video.

- Wednesday: one routine or workout clip.

- Thursday: one beginner guide.

- Friday: one form tutorial on a second exercise.

- Saturday: one progress, mindset, or gym life video.

- Sunday: rest, or repost the best performer of the week with a new hook.

## Fill rules

- Rotate body parts across the week so the same muscle is not taught on back to back days.

- Repeat a winning format with a new exercise before inventing a new format.

- Leave two empty slots per month for questions and requests from comments.

- Mark each slot with topic, mode, platform, length, and status as planned, filmed, or posted.
